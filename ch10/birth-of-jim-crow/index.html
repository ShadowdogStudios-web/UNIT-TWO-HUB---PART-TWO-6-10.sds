Source · the tools of Jim Crow

How rights were hollowed out

To take the vote: poll taxes, literacy tests, grandfather clauses — none mentioning race, but all aimed at Black voters (registration fell 90%+ in some states).
To enforce separation: segregation laws in schools, transit, and public life, upheld as “separate but equal” in Plessy v. Ferguson (1896). — summarized from the historical record. Public domain.
