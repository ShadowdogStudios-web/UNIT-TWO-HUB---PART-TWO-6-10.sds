Source · the clause and the system

From eleven words to a system

13th Amendment: “Neither slavery nor involuntary servitude, except as a punishment for crime whereof the party shall have been duly convicted …”

Convict leasing: states used vague laws (vagrancy, loitering) to convict Black Southerners, then leased them to companies for unpaid labor in mines, fields, and railroads — often under conditions deadlier than slavery. — summarized from the historical record. Public domain.
