Source · what the Enforcement Acts did

Federal power against terror

The Enforcement Acts (1870–71), including the Ku Klux Klan Act, made it a federal crime to conspire to deny citizens their rights; let the president use troops and suspend habeas corpus; and led to hundreds of prosecutions. By 1872 the Klan was largely broken and Black voting rebounded. — summarized from the historical record. Public domain.
