What the Court ruled

The Supreme Court overturned the convictions of Colfax attackers, holding that the Fourteenth Amendment restrained only state action, not the acts of private individuals — so protecting citizens from private mob violence was a matter for the states, not the federal government. — summary of United States v. Cruikshank (1876). Public domain.
