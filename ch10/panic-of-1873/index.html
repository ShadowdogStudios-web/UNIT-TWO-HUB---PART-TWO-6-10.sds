From Wall Street to the South

Overbuilt railroads → the failure of Jay Cooke & Co. (Sept. 1873) → stock market closes, thousands of businesses fail, mass unemployment → Northern voters focus on the economy → in 1874, Democrats win control of the House → support for enforcing Reconstruction collapses. — summarized from the historical record.
