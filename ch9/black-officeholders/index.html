The numbers

~2,000 Black men held office during Reconstruction · 16 served in Congress · Hiram Revels (1870) was the first Black U.S. Senator · Blanche K. Bruce served a full Senate term · for a time Black representatives were a majority in South Carolina's lower house. — summarized from the historical record. Public domain.
