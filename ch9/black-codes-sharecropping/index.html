Source · how sharecropping worked

The debt cycle

A sharecropper farmed a landowner's land for a share of the crop. He borrowed — at high interest — for seed, tools, and food against the coming harvest. At settlement, the landowner's accounts often showed the cropper still owed money. The law then required him to keep working to pay the debt. The next year began the same way. — summarized from the historical record. Public domain.
