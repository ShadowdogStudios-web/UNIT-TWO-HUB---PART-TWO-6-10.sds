The institutions of freedom

Independent Black churches (the AME church grew rapidly) · thousands of schools, and the first HBCUs (Howard, Fisk, Hampton) · newspaper ads and long journeys to reunite scattered families · legalized marriages · mutual-aid and benevolent societies · Black-owned businesses. — summarized from the historical record. Public domain.
