“The Freedmen's Bureau”

“The Freedmen's Bureau” — wood engraving by Alfred R. Waud, Harper's Weekly, 1868, public domain (Library of Congress).
Source · the Bureau's record

Achievements and limits

Achievements: millions of food rations; ~4,000 schools; foundations of Black colleges; labor contracts enforced; families reunited; marriages legalized.
Limits: chronically underfunded and understaffed; fierce white resistance and violence; the promise of land mostly broken; shut down by 1872. — summarized from the historical record. Public domain.
